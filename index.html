#01

Eiffel Tower

📍 Paris, France

The iconic iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars is a global cultural icon of France and one of the most recognizable structures in the world. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it stands 330 meters tall and has welcomed over 300 million visitors since it opened.

👥 7M+ visitors / year 🗼 Built 1889 📏 330 m tall
#02

Great Wall of China

📍 Huairou, China

A series of fortifications stretching over 21,000 km across northern China, built over 2,000 years ago to protect against invasions. It's the longest wall in the world and one of the New Seven Wonders.

👥 10M+ visitors / year 🏯 Built 7th century BC 📏 21,196 km long
#03

Colosseum

📍 Rome, Italy

The largest ancient amphitheatre ever built, hosting gladiator contests and public spectacles in the heart of the Roman Empire. It could hold up to 80,000 spectators and remains the most iconic symbol of Imperial Rome.

👥 7.6M+ visitors / year 🏛️ Built 80 AD 🎭 Capacity 80,000
#04

Taj Mahal

📍 Agra, India

A stunning white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site and widely considered the finest example of Mughal architecture.

👥 7.5M+ visitors / year 🕌 Built 1653 💎 UNESCO Site
#05

Machu Picchu

📍 Cusco Region, Peru

A 15th-century Inca citadel set high in the Andes Mountains at 2,430 meters. Rediscovered in 1911, it's one of the New Seven Wonders of the World and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

👥 1.5M+ visitors / year 🏔️ Built 1450 ⛰️ 2,430 m elevation
#06

Statue of Liberty

📍 New York, USA

A colossal neoclassical sculpture on Liberty Island — a universal symbol of freedom and democracy. Gifted by France in 1886, it stands 93 meters tall including the pedestal.

👥 4.5M+ visitors / year 🗽 Built 1886 🎁 Gift from France
#07

Christ the Redeemer

📍 Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

An Art Deco statue of Jesus Christ atop the Corcovado mountain, overlooking the vibrant city of Rio. It stands 30 meters tall and is one of the New Seven Wonders of the World.

👥 2M+ visitors / year ⛪ Built 1931 📏 30 m tall
#08

Pyramids of Giza

📍 Giza, Egypt

The oldest of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World — monumental tombs built for pharaohs over 4,500 years ago. The Great Pyramid of Giza was the tallest man-made structure for over 3,800 years.

👥 14.7M+ visitors / year 🔺 Built 2560 BC 📏 146 m tall
#09

Burj Khalifa

📍 Dubai, UAE

The world's tallest building at 828 meters with 163 floors. An engineering marvel with an observation deck on the 148th floor — visitors can see the Persian Gulf and beyond from the top.

👥 2M+ visitors / year 🏙️ Built 2010 📏 828 m tall
#10

Sydney Opera House

📍 Sydney, Australia

A multi-venue performing arts centre with a distinctive sail-like design — a masterpiece of 20th-century architecture and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It hosts over 1,500 performances a year.

👥 10.9M+ visitors / year 🎭 Built 1973 💎 UNESCO Site
